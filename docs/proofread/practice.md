## SUSPECT

- `penalties-late-filing-late-payment-and-inaccuracies.ts` (SA table and Hana scenario): GOV.UK says the new points and late payment penalties apply "from April 2027" to everyone filing a personal SA return. It does not say whether that means the 2027/28 tax year or returns and payments due after April 2027. Hana's 2025/26 return (due 31 January 2027, filed 15 August 2027) is treated under the traditional regime, which is very probably right, but the page doesn't settle it.
- `engagement-letters...ts` / `hmrc-agent-authorisation...ts` ("only one agent per service"; "HMRC may only be able to deal with one"): this is cautious wording, but HMRC guidance on whether several agents can hold authorisation for the same service varies by service. It is not wrong as phrased, so left unchanged.
