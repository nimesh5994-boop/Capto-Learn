The worker is not a director of the fee-payer, and ESM10019 says the usual earnings-period rules apply based on when the fee-payer pays (monthly payments mean a monthly period). A reconciliation from rate to pay is good practice, but I found no source making it mandatory (the agency's Key Information Document is the closest duty).
