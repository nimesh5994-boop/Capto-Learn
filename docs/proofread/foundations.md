Their salary is taxed through PAYE as employment income, but a director isn't necessarily an employee in law. The GOV.UK MLR page I fetched supports internal reporting to the nominated officer. ## Confirmed against official sources this session (no change needed)

- Records: self-employed records kept at least 5 years after the 31 January deadline, and estimates must be flagged to HMRC (GOV.UK *Business records if you're self-employed*). Company records kept for 6 years from the end of the financial year (GOV.UK). CT records kept to the sixth anniversary of the end of the accounting period, and s388's 3 years (private) and 6 years (plc) (CH14600). Company records must include stock at the year end and the stocktakes behind it.
- Cash basis is the default from 2024/25 for sole traders and partnerships without corporate partners, and limited companies, LLPs and partnerships with corporate partners can't use it (GOV.UK *Cash basis*).
- CT rates for FY2026 and FY2025: small profits rate 19% (up to £50,000), main rate 25% (over £250,000), marginal relief in between. Payment due 9 months and 1 day after the period end.
- Companies House: private company accounts due 9 months after the ARD. First accounts due 21 months from incorporation or 3 months from the ARD, whichever is longer. Micro thresholds £1m / £500,000 / 10 employees and small thresholds £15m / £7.5m / 50 employees for periods beginning on or after 6 April 2025.
- VAT standard rate 20%. Bad debt relief: 6 months from the later of the due date and the supply date, 4 years 6 months time limit, claimed in box 4, and not needed under cash accounting (Notice 700/18).
- Bad and doubtful debts for tax: specific provisions only and general reserves not admitted (ITTOIA s35), and companies' trade debts fall under the loan relationship rules (BIM42701).
- AIA £1m, cars excluded, structures and buildings not plant (GOV.UK *Capital allowances*). Replacing single glazing with double glazing is now a repair (BIM46925).
- FRS 102 (September 2024 edition, FRC): accrual basis (2.48); PPE cost includes directly attributable costs and non-refundable taxes and excludes staff training and overheads (17.10, 17.11); land not usually depreciated, depreciable amount allocated systematically, residual value and useful life reviewed when indicators exist (17.16, 17.18, 17.19); inventories at the lower of cost and estimated selling price less costs to complete and sell, FIFO or weighted average, LIFO not permitted (13.4, 13.18), item-by-item impairment (27.3); impairment of financial assets on objective evidence (11.21); material prior period errors corrected retrospectively (10.21). HMRC follows FIFO not LIFO (BIM33120).
- Every other cited HMRC manual page loads and its title matches its label (BIM31020, BIM31045, BIM31060, BIM33000, BIM33115, BIM35000, BIM42201, BIM42705, BIM46900, CA20006, EM3000).
